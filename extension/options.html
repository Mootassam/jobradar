JobRadar Autofill

Connect the extension to your JobRadar. Your profile and CV stay on your computer.

Forms embedded from job systems

Some career pages show the application form inside a frame from Greenhouse, Lever or others. To fill those too, allow the extension to read forms on the pages you choose to fill. It still only runs when you click it.

How to use

  1. Open a job application page.
  2. Click the JobRadar icon in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+F.
  3. Check every filled field (green), answer the ones in amber, then click the site's own Submit button.
  4. Click Mark as applied - the job moves to Applied in your JobRadar pipeline.
